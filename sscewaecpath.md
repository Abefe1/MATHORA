# Comprehensive WAEC SSCE Mathematics & Further Mathematics Study Path

---

## 🎯 Executive Overview & WAEC Exam Structure

This document synthesizes the official **Nigerian Senior Secondary School (SS1 – SS3) Curriculum (NERDC Aligned)** and **West African Examinations Council (WAEC SSCE) Examination Syllabus** into a unified, step-by-step 3-Year Mastery Path.

### 📜 WAEC SSCE Mathematics Examination Blueprint
- **Paper 1 (Multiple Choice Questions - MCQ)**
  - **Format**: 50 objective questions covering all SS1–SS3 topics.
  - **Duration**: 1 Hour 30 Minutes.
  - **Weighting**: 40% of total score.
- **Paper 2 (Essay / Theory)**
  - **Duration**: 2 Hours 30 Minutes.
  - **Weighting**: 60% of total score.
  - **Section A (Compulsory Core)**: 5 core questions covering foundational algebra, mensuration, and geometry (40 Marks).
  - **Section B (Advanced Options)**: 5 selected questions out of 8 covering trigonometry, statistics, circle theorems, longitudes/latitudes, and vectors (60 Marks).

## 🟢 YEAR 1: SENIOR SECONDARY 1 (SS1) — FOUNDATIONAL YEAR

### 📘 SS1 TERM 1: Number Systems & Fundamental Algebra
- **Week 1: Number Base Systems**
  - Binary (Base 2), Octal (Base 8), Decimal (Base 10), and Hexadecimal (Base 16).
  - Conversion between non-decimal bases and base 10.
  - Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division in base 2 and base 5.
- **Week 2: Modular Arithmetic**
  - Concept of modulo arithmetic ($a \pmod m$).
  - Addition, subtraction, and multiplication tables in modular systems.
  - Application to days of the week, clocks, and recurring calendars.
- **Week 3: Sets & Venn Diagrams**
  - Definition of sets, elements, universal set, empty set, subsets, and power sets.
  - Set operations: Union ($\cup$), Intersection ($\cap$), Complement ($A'$), Difference ($A \setminus B$).
  - Venn diagrams with 2 and 3 sets; word problems involving survey data.
- **Week 4: Indices & Laws of Exponents**
  - Multiplication law ($a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$), Division law ($a^m \div a^n = a^{m-n}$), Power law ($(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$).
  - Zero index ($a^0 = 1$), Negative index ($a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$), Fractional index ($a^{m/n} = \sqrt[n]{a^m}$).
  - Solving exponential equations (e.g., $2^{x+1} = 16$).
- **Week 5: Logarithms of Numbers Greater Than 1**
  - Concept of logarithms as inverse of indices ($\log_b a = c \iff b^c = a$).
  - Laws of logarithms: Product, Quotient, Power, and Change of Base.
  - Using 4-figure tables for multiplication, division, powers, and roots of numbers $> 1$.
- **Week 6: Review of Half-Term Work & Periodic Assessment**
- **Week 7: Simple Equations & Change of Subject of Formula**
  - Linear equations in one variable; word problems leading to linear equations.
  - Changing the subject of a formula involving roots, powers, and fractions.
- **Week 8: Algebraic Fractions**
  - Simplification of rational expressions.
  - Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division of algebraic fractions.
  - Equations involving algebraic fractions.
- **Week 9: Variations**
  - Direct Variation ($y \propto x \implies y = kx$).
  - Inverse Variation ($y \propto \frac{1}{x} \implies y = \frac{k}{x}$).
  - Joint Variation ($y \propto xz \implies y = kxz$).
  - Partial Variation ($y = k_1 x + k_2$).
- **Week 10 – 12: Revision & First Term Examination**

### 📙 SS1 TERM 2: Quadratics, Functions & Inequalities
- **Week 1: Quadratic Equations — Factorization Method**
  - Standard form of quadratic equations ($ax^2 + bx + c = 0$).
  - Factorizing quadratic expressions by grouping and decomposition.
  - Difference of two squares ($a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$).
- **Week 2: Quadratic Equations — Completing the Square & Formula**
  - Derivation of the quadratic formula ($x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$).
  - Solving quadratic equations using completing the square.
  - Application of the quadratic formula to real roots.
- **Week 3: Graphical Solution of Quadratic Equations**
  - Constructing tables of values for quadratic functions ($y = ax^2 + bx + c$).
  - Plotting quadratic curves on Cartesian coordinate grids.
  - Reading roots, minimum/maximum turning points, and line of symmetry from graphs.
- **Week 4: Linear Inequalities in One Variable**
  - Inequality symbols ($>, <, \ge, \le$).
  - Solving linear inequalities and representing solutions on number lines.
  - Reversing inequality signs when multiplying/dividing by negative numbers.
- **Week 5: Linear Inequalities in Two Variables**
  - Graphing linear inequalities on Cartesian planes.
  - Shading required regions and finding feasible solution regions.
- **Week 6: Review of Half-Term Work & Periodic Assessment**
- **Week 7: Introduction to Functions & Relations**
  - Definition of relation, domain, co-domain, and range.
  - Types of mappings: One-to-one, many-to-one, onto, and inverse functions.
- **Week 8: Sequence & Series (Introduction to A.P.)**
  - Definition of sequences, terms, and general rule ($T_n$).
  - Arithmetic Progression (A.P.): First term ($a$), common difference ($d$), $n$-th term ($T_n = a + (n-1)d$).
- **Week 9: Sum of Arithmetic Progression**
  - Sum of first $n$ terms of an A.P. ($S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$ or $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[a + l]$).
  - Word problems on A.P. in real-world scenarios.
- **Week 10 – 12: Revision & Second Term Examination**

### 📕 SS1 TERM 3: Geometry, Mensuration & Statistics
- **Week 1: Plane Geometry — Triangles & Polygons**
  - Sum of interior angles of a triangle ($180^\circ$) and polygon ($(n-2) \times 180^\circ$).
  - Exterior angle theorem of a triangle and sum of exterior angles of any polygon ($360^\circ$).
  - Types of polygons: Pentagons, hexagons, octagons, regular vs irregular polygons.
- **Week 2: Circle Geometry — Angles at Center & Circumference**
  - Theorem 1: Angle subtended by an arc at the center is twice the angle subtended at the circumference.
  - Theorem 2: Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal.
  - Theorem 3: Angle in a semi-circle is a right angle ($90^\circ$).
- **Week 3: Circle Geometry — Cyclic Quadrilaterals & Tangents**
  - Theorem 4: Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary ($A + C = 180^\circ$).
  - Theorem 5: Tangent to a circle is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.
  - Theorem 6: Tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal in length.
- **Week 4: Trigonometric Ratios of Acute Angles**
  - Right-angled triangle ratios: Sine, Cosine, and Tangent (SOH CAH TOA).
  - Reciprocal ratios: Cosecant ($\csc$), Secant ($\sec$), and Cotangent ($\cot$).
- **Week 5: Special Angles & Angles of Elevation / Depression**
  - Exact trigonometric ratios for $30^\circ$, $45^\circ$, and $60^\circ$.
  - Angles of elevation (looking up) and depression (looking down).
  - Solving height and distance problems using right-angled triangles.
- **Week 6: Review of Half-Term Work & Periodic Assessment**
- **Week 7: Mensuration — Plane Shapes**
  - Perimeter and area of triangles, rectangles, parallelograms, trapeziums, and rhombuses.
  - Length of arc ($L = \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r$) and area of sector ($A = \frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2$).
  - Area of segment of a circle.
- **Week 8: Mensuration — Solid Shapes**
  - Surface area and volume of cubes, cuboids, cylinders, and prisms.
  - Surface area and volume of cones and spheres.
- **Week 9: Statistics — Ungrouped Data**
  - Frequency distribution tables for ungrouped data.
  - Measures of central tendency: Mean ($\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x}{n}$), Median, and Mode.
  - Graphical representation: Bar charts, pie charts, and pictograms.
- **Week 10 – 12: Revision & Third Term Examination**

## 🟡 YEAR 2: SENIOR SECONDARY 2 (SS2) — CORE WAEC MASTERY YEAR

### 📘 SS2 TERM 1: Advanced Logarithms, Progressions & Simultaneous Equations
- **Week 1: Logarithms of Numbers Less Than 1**
  - Characteristics and mantissa of numbers $< 1$ (negative characteristics e.g., $\bar{1}, \bar{2}$).
  - Logarithmic calculations involving multiplication, division, powers, and roots using 4-figure tables.
  - Reciprocals of numbers using tables and standard calculations.
- **Week 2: Approximations, Standard Form & Percentage Error**
  - Rounding to decimal places and significant figures.
  - Expressing numbers in scientific standard form ($a \times 10^n$, $1 \le a < 10$).
  - Calculating Percentage Error ($\text{Percentage Error} = \frac{|\text{Estimated Value} - \text{Actual Value}|}{\text{Actual Value}} \times 100\%$).
- **Week 3: Sequences & Series — Geometric Progression (G.P.)**
  - Concept of G.P., first term ($a$), common ratio ($r = \frac{T_2}{T_1}$).
  - $n$-th term of a G.P. ($T_n = ar^{n-1}$).
  - Finding geometric means between two numbers.
- **Week 4: Sum of Geometric Progression & Sum to Infinity**
  - Sum of first $n$ terms of a G.P. ($S_n = \frac{a(1-r^n)}{1-r}$ for $|r| < 1$, $S_n = \frac{a(r^n-1)}{r-1}$ for $|r| > 1$).
  - Sum to infinity of a convergent G.P. ($S_\infty = \frac{a}{1-r}$, $|r| < 1$).
  - Real-world applications of G.P. (compound interest, population growth).
- **Week 5: Construction of Quadratic Equations from Roots**
  - Formula: $x^2 - (\text{Sum of Roots})x + (\text{Product of Roots}) = 0$.
  - Given roots $\alpha$ and $\beta$, forming equations for $\alpha+\beta$ and $\alpha\beta$.
  - Word problems leading to quadratic equations.
- **Week 6: Review of Half-Term Work & Periodic Assessment**
- **Week 7: Simultaneous Equations — Linear Systems**
  - Solving 2-variable linear simultaneous equations via Elimination and Substitution.
  - Matrix determinant method (Cramer's Rule preview).
  - Word problems leading to simultaneous linear equations.
- **Week 8: Simultaneous Equations — One Linear & One Quadratic**
  - Substitution method for solving non-linear systems ($y = mx + c$ and $ax^2 + by^2 = d$).
  - Graphical method for finding points of intersection between a line and a curve.
- **Week 9: Straight Line Graphs & Gradients**
  - Gradient of a straight line ($m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$).
  - Equation of a straight line: Slope-intercept form ($y = mx + c$), Point-slope form ($y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$).
  - Parallel lines ($m_1 = m_2$) and Perpendicular lines ($m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1$).
  - Gradient of a curve at a point using tangents.
- **Week 10 – 12: Revision & First Term Examination**

### 📙 SS2 TERM 2: Advanced Trigonometry, Bearings & Circle Geometry
- **Week 1: Trigonometric Ratios in All Four Quadrants**
  - Unit circle and CAST rule (Quadrant I: All positive, II: Sine, III: Tangent, IV: Cosine).
  - Trigonometric ratios for angles from $0^\circ$ to $360^\circ$ and negative angles.
  - Solving simple trigonometric equations (e.g., $\sin \theta = 0.5$).
- **Week 2: Sine Rule & Cosine Rule**
  - Derivation and application of the Sine Rule ($\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$).
  - Derivation and application of the Cosine Rule ($a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc \cos A$).
  - Area of non-right-angled triangles ($A = \frac{1}{2}ab \sin C$).
- **Week 3: Angles of Elevation, Depression & 3D Trigonometry**
  - Heights and distances involving two observations.
  - Three-dimensional trigonometric problems involving cuboids and pyramids.
- **Week 4: Bearings & Distances**
  - Cardinal points vs 3-figure bearings (measured clockwise from True North $000^\circ$ to $360^\circ$).
  - Forward bearing vs Reverse bearing (Difference of $180^\circ$).
  - Solving bearing problems using sketch diagrams, Sine Rule, and Cosine Rule.
- **Week 5: Advanced Circle Geometry — Tangents & Alternate Segment**
  - Alternate Segment Theorem: Angle between a tangent and a chord equals the angle subtended by the chord in the alternate segment.
  - Intersecting chords theorem (internal and external intersection of chords).
- **Week 6: Review of Half-Term Work & Periodic Assessment**
- **Week 7: Geometric Constructions**
  - Bisecting line segments and angles ($60^\circ, 30^\circ, 90^\circ, 45^\circ, 75^\circ, 105^\circ, 135^\circ, 150^\circ$).
  - Constructing triangles, quadrilaterals, and regular polygons with ruler and pair of compasses only.
- **Week 8: Concept of Loci (Plural of Locus)**
  - Locus of points equidistant from two fixed points (perpendicular bisector).
  - Locus of points equidistant from two intersecting lines (angle bisector).
  - Locus of points at a given distance from a fixed point (circle) or a straight line (parallel lines).
  - Intersections of loci in construction problems.
- **Week 9: Logical Reasoning**
  - Simple and compound statements.
  - Conjunction ($\land$), Disjunction ($\lor$), Implication ($\implies$), and Equivalence ($\iff$).
  - Truth tables, negation ($\sim$), converse, inverse, and contrapositive statements.
- **Week 10 – 12: Revision & Second Term Examination**

### 📕 SS2 TERM 3: Global Mensuration, Advanced Statistics & Probability
- **Week 1: Mensuration of Solid Shapes — Frustums**
  - Frustum of a cone and pyramid.
  - Calculating volume, total surface area, and slant height of frustums.
- **Week 2: Earth as a Sphere — Latitudes & Longitudes**
  - Position of places on the Earth's surface using Latitudes ($\theta^\circ\text{N/S}$) and Longitudes ($\phi^\circ\text{E/W}$).
  - Great circles (Equator, Meridians) and Small circles (Parallels of latitude).
  - Radius of parallel of latitude ($r = R \cos \theta$).
- **Week 3: Earth as a Sphere — Distances along Great & Small Circles**
  - Distance along a meridian or Equator ($d = \frac{\alpha}{360} \times 2\pi R$).
  - Distance along a parallel of latitude ($d = \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r = \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi R \cos \phi$).
  - Speed in knots and distance in nautical miles ($1\text{ nautical mile} = 1\text{ minute of arc} = 60\text{ nautical miles per degree}$).
- **Week 4: Statistics — Grouped Data & Frequency Tables**
  - Class intervals, class boundaries, class marks (midpoints), and class widths.
  - Constructing grouped frequency distribution tables.
  - Histograms with equal and unequal class intervals; frequency polygons.
- **Week 5: Measures of Central Tendency for Grouped Data**
  - Mean of grouped data ($\bar{x} = \frac{\sum fx}{\sum f}$ or Assumed Mean method $\bar{x} = A + \frac{\sum fd}{\sum f}$).
  - Median of grouped data ($L_m + \left[\frac{\frac{N}{2} - F_b}{f_m}\right]c$).
  - Mode of grouped data ($L_o + \left[\frac{\Delta_1}{\Delta_1 + \Delta_2}\right]c$).
- **Week 6: Review of Half-Term Work & Periodic Assessment**
- **Week 7: Cumulative Frequency Curve (Ogive)**
  - Constructing cumulative frequency tables.
  - Drawing Ogive curves (Cumulative Frequency vs Upper Class Boundaries).
  - Estimating Median, Lower Quartile ($Q_1$), Upper Quartile ($Q_3$), Interquartile Range ($Q_3 - Q_1$), Semi-interquartile Range, and Percentiles from Ogive graphs.
- **Week 8: Measures of Dispersion**
  - Range and Mean Deviation ($\frac{\sum f|x - \bar{x}|}{\sum f}$).
  - Variance ($\sigma^2 = \frac{\sum f(x - \bar{x})^2}{\sum f}$) and Standard Deviation ($\sigma = \sqrt{\text{Variance}}$).
- **Week 9: Probability — Single & Combined Events**
  - Sample space, events, and probability formula ($P(E) = \frac{n(E)}{n(S)}$).
  - Addition law for mutually exclusive events ($P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B)$).
  - Multiplication law for independent events ($P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)$).
  - Tree diagrams for conditional probability and sampling without replacement.
- **Week 10 – 12: Revision & Third Term Examination**

  - Solving simultaneous linear equations using matrix inverse method.
- **Week 3: Vectors in Two Dimensions (2D)**
  - Scalar vs Vector quantities.
  - Vector representation: Displacement vectors ($\vec{AB}$), position vectors ($\mathbf{r} = x\mathbf{i} + y\mathbf{j}$).

  - Simplification of surds ($\sqrt{ab} = \sqrt{a} \times \sqrt{b}$).

- **Week 6: Review of Half-Term Work & Periodic Assessment**
- **Week 7: Linear Programming & Feasible Regions**
  - Formulating linear inequalities from word problems.
  - Graphing multiple linear inequalities to define feasible solution regions.
  - Finding maximum and minimum values of an objective function ($Z = ax + by$) at corner points.
- **Week 8: Commercial Mathematics — Taxes, Interest & Stocks**
  - Compound interest calculations using formula ($A = P(1 + \frac{r}{100})^n$).
  - Depreciation and inflation.
  - Personal income tax calculations (taxable income, tax allowances, tax rates).

## 🟣 FURTHER MATHEMATICS TRACK (OPTIONAL HIGH-ACHIEVER MODULES)

For students taking **WAEC Further Mathematics**, the following modules extend the core curriculum:

### 1. Pure Mathematics
- **Functions**: Polynomials, remainder & factor theorems, rational functions, partial fractions.
- **Calculus — Differentiation**:
  - Limits of functions ($\lim_{x \to a} f(x)$).
  - Differentiation from First Principles ($f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$).
  - Rules of differentiation: Product Rule ($(uv)' = u'v + uv'$), Quotient Rule ($(\frac{u}{v})' = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}$), Chain Rule ($\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{du} \cdot \frac{du}{dx}$).
  - Derivatives of trigonometric functions ($\sin x \to \cos x$, $\cos x \to -\sin x$), exponential ($e^{ax}$), and logarithmic ($\ln x$) functions.
  - Applications: Rate of change, equations of tangents & normals, turning points (maxima & minima).
- **Calculus — Integration**:
  - Integration as reverse of differentiation.
  - Standard integrals ($\int x^n dx$, $\int \sin x dx$, $\int e^{ax} dx$).
  - Definite integrals and Area under curves ($\int_a^b f(x) dx$).
- **Conic Sections**:
  - Equations of circles ($(x-a)^2 + (y-b)^2 = r^2$, general form $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$).
  - Tangents and normals to circles.
  - Parabolas ($y^2 = 4ax$), Ellipses ($\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$), and Hyperbolas.

### 2. Statistics & Probability
- **Permutations & Combinations**:
  - Factorial notation ($n!$).
  - Permutations ($^nP_r = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!}$).
  - Combinations ($^nC_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$).
- **Probability Distributions**:
  - Binomial Distribution ($P(X = k) = ^nC_k p^k (1-p)^{n-k}$).
  - Normal Distribution ($Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma}$) and standard normal tables.

### 3. Vectors & Mechanics
- **Vectors in 3D**: Position vectors, scalar product ($\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}$), vector product ($\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}$).
- **Statics**: Resolution of forces, equilibrium of coplanar forces, moments of forces, friction.
- **Dynamics**: Newton's laws of motion, work ($W = F \cdot s$), energy (Kinetic $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$, Potential $E_p = mgh$), power ($P = Fv$), impulse ($I = F \Delta t$), and momentum ($p = mv$).

## ⚡ WAEC EXAM SHORTCUTS & MISCONCEPTION RESCUE GUIDE

### 1. Quadratic Roots Shortcut
For quadratic equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$:
- **Sum of roots**: $\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a}$
- **Product of roots**: $\alpha \cdot \beta = \frac{c}{a}$
- *WAEC MCQ Trick*: If asked for $x^2 + 5x + 6 = 0$, test $\alpha+\beta = -5$ and $\alpha\beta = 6 \implies$ roots are $-2$ and $-3$.

### 2. Logarithm Characteristic Rescue
- For numbers $> 1$: Characteristic = $(\text{Number of digits before decimal}) - 1$.
  - Example: $432.5 \implies 3 - 1 = 2 \implies \log = 2.6360$.
- For numbers $< 1$: Characteristic = $-(\text{Number of zeros immediately after decimal} + 1)$.
  - Example: $0.00432 \implies -(2 + 1) = \bar{3} \implies \log = \bar{3}.6360$.

### 3. Negative Sign Expansion Trap
- **Common Trap**: Expanding $-(x - 3)^2 \neq -(x^2 - 6x + 9) \neq -x^2 - 6x - 9$.
- **Correct Method**: Always expand inside brackets first: $-(x - 3)^2 = -(x^2 - 6x + 9) = -x^2 + 6x - 9$.

### 4. Trigonometric Sign Rules (CAST)
- **Quadrant I ($0^\circ - 90^\circ$)**: All positive ($+ \sin, + \cos, + \tan$).
- **Quadrant II ($90^\circ - 180^\circ$)**: Sine positive ($+ \sin$, $-\cos, -\tan$).
- **Quadrant III ($180^\circ - 270^\circ$)**: Tangent positive ($+ \tan$, $-\sin, -\cos$).
- **Quadrant IV ($270^\circ - 360^\circ$)**: Cosine positive ($+ \cos$, $-\sin, -\tan$).
