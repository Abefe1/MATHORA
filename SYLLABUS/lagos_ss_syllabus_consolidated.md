# Lagos State SS Mathematics & Further Mathematics Syllabus (Consolidated)

This document consolidates the week-by-week **scheme of work** (topic lists only — not the full lesson notes/worked examples) from the Lagos State Ministry of Education SS1–SS3 e-learning note documents, for both **Mathematics** (General/Core) and **Further Mathematics**. It was compiled from 19 source Word documents (some of which were exact duplicates or overlapping/loosely-labelled files); the clearly per-term-labelled files were treated as the primary source of truth, with unlabelled files ("MATHss1.docx", "MATHS.docx", "MATHEMATICS.docx", "FURTHER MATHS.docx") used only to fill gaps where no dedicated per-term file existed. Three originally-missing Further Mathematics terms (SS1 First Term, SS2 First Term, SS3 First Term) were later supplied and added. Wording has been lightly cleaned up (removing stray line breaks, repeated words, and footnote/page-number artifacts introduced by the Word-to-text extraction) without altering the underlying mathematical content.

Where a source file's week numbering was lost or garbled in extraction, this is noted explicitly under that term.

## MATHEMATICS

### SS1

#### First Term
*Source: MATHss1.docx (no dedicated "1ST TERM S1 MATHEMATICS" file existed; this is the primary/only source).*

- Week 1: Revision of JSS3 work and basic operations on integers — addition, subtraction, multiplication and division.
- Week 2: Number Bases — (a) conversion from one base to base ten, (b) conversion of decimal fractions ("bicimals") in one base to base ten, (c) conversion of numbers from one base to another.
- Week 3: Number Bases (continued) — (a) addition, subtraction, multiplication and division of numbers in different bases, (b) application of number bases to computer programming.
- Week 4: Concept of Modular Arithmetic — addition, subtraction, multiplication and division operations in modular arithmetic.
- Week 5: Standard Form and Approximation.
- Week 6: Indices — (a) application of laws of indices, (b) negative, zero and fractional indices.
- Week 7: Review of first half term's work and periodic test.
- Week 8: Logarithms of numbers greater than 1 (whole numbers) — use of logarithm tables for multiplication and division of numbers.
- Week 9: Logarithms (continued) — (a) calculations involving powers and roots, (b) relationship between indices and logarithms.
- Week 10: (a) Simple equations and variation, (b) change of subject of formulae, (c) types of variation — direct, inverse, joint and partial, (d) application of variation to practical problems.
- Week 11: Revision.
- Week 12–13: Examination.

**Reference Books:**
- MAN Mathematics for Senior Secondary Schools 1
- Essential Mathematics for Senior Secondary Schools 1 by A.J.S. Oluwasanmi
- New General Mathematics for Senior Secondary School 1 by M.F. Macrae et al.

#### Second Term
*Source: "2ND TERM S1 MATHEMATICS.docx". Note: in the source document the week numbers were lost during extraction (topics ran together as an unbroken list of phrases). The topic sequence below has been reconstructed from the content order and standard term structure (11 weeks including a half-term review); week numbers are therefore inferred, not verbatim from the source.*

- Week 1: Quadratic Equations by (a) factorisation, (b) completing the square method.
- Week 2: General form of a quadratic equation leading to the formula method, from ax² + bx + c = 0.
- Week 3: Solving quadratic equations by graphical methods — reading the roots from the graph; determination of minimum and maximum values; line of symmetry.
- Week 4: Idea of Sets — universal sets, finite and infinite sets, empty set, subsets.
- Week 5: Notation for union and intersection of sets; complements of sets; disjoint/null sets.
- Week 6: Venn diagrams and their use in solving problems involving two and three sets, in relation to real-life situations.
- Week 7: Review of first half term's work and periodic test.
- Week 8: Trigonometric Ratios — sine, cosine, tangent of acute angles; use of tables of trigonometric ratios; determination of length of chord using trigonometric ratios.
- Week 9: Graph of sine and cosine for angles 0°–360°; (a) application of sine, cosine and tangent to simple problems on right-angled triangles, (b) angles of elevation and depression, (c) bearing and distances of places (strict application of trigonometric ratios).
- Week 10: (a) Introduction to circles and their properties, (b) calculation of length of arc and perimeter of a sector, (c) area of sectors and segments; area of triangles.
- Week 11: Logic — simple true and false statements; negation and contrapositive of a simple statement; antecedent, consequent and conditional statements (implication).

**Reference Books:**
- New General Mathematics SSS1 by M.F. Macrae et al.
- WABP Essential Mathematics for Senior Secondary Schools 1 by A.J.S. Oluwasanmi

#### Third Term
*Source: "3RD TERM S1 MATHEMATICS.docx". Note: as with SS1 Second Term, individual week numbers were lost in extraction; the topic sequence below preserves the source's content order.*

- Mensuration — the concept of 3-D shapes: cube, cuboid, cylinder, triangular prism, cone, rectangular-based pyramid; total surface area of cone and cylinder and their volumes.
- (a) Volumes of frustums of cones, rectangular-based pyramids and other pyramids, (b) proof that the angle sum of a triangle = 180°, (c) the exterior angle of a triangle.
- Geometrical Construction — revision of construction of triangles; drawing and bisection of a line segment; construction and bisection of angles 90°, 45°, 135°, 22½°, 57½°.
- Construction and bisection of angles: 30°, 60°, 90°, 120°, 150°, etc.
- Construction of a quadrilateral polygon (a four-sided figure with given conditions, e.g. a parallelogram); construction of an equilateral triangle.
- Locus of moving points, including equidistance from two lines/two points, and constant distance from a point.
- Deductive proof: sum of angles of a triangle; relationship of triangles on a straight line.
- Revision of angles on parallel lines cut by a transversal; congruent triangles; properties of a parallelogram and the intercept theorem.
- Statistics — collection, tabulation and presentation of data (e.g. heights, ages, weights, test/exam scores, school/class populations, animal species, vehicle types, etc.).
- Calculation of range, median and mode of ungrouped data (using data collected by students or from other statistical records).
- Revision of collection, tabulation and presentation of grouped data (heights, ages, weights, test/exam scores, class populations).
- Calculation of range, median and mode of grouped data.
- Statistical graphs — drawing of bar chart, pie chart and histogram; cumulative frequency curve; reading and drawing inferences from graphs.
- Mean deviation, variance and standard deviation of grouped data, applied to practical/real-life problems.
- Revision/Examination.

**Reference Books:**
- New General Mathematics SSS1 by M.F. Macrae et al.
- Essential Mathematics SS1

### SS2

#### First Term
*Source: "1ST TERM S2 MATHEMATICS.docx".*

- Week 1: Revision of logarithm of numbers greater than one and logarithm of numbers less than one; reciprocals and accuracy of results using standard calculation.
- Week 2: Approximations; calculations using standard form; significant figures; percentage error.
- Week 3: Sequence and Series — concept of sequence and series; terms of Arithmetic Progressions (A.P.) and their sum; solving problems on A.P.
- Week 4: Geometric Progressions — the nth term and sum of the first n terms; problem solving on G.P. and geometric mean.
- Week 5: Construction of a quadratic equation from the sum and product of roots; word problems leading to quadratic equations.
- Week 6: Review of half term work and periodic test.
- Week 7: Simultaneous Equations — solving by elimination and substitution methods; word problems leading to simultaneous equations.
- Week 8: Simultaneous Equations — solving equations involving one linear and one quadratic equation; using graphical methods to solve quadratic equations.
- Week 9: Straight Line Graphs — gradient of a straight line; gradient of a curve; drawing tangents to a curve.
- Week 10: Revision.

**Reference Books:**
- New General Mathematics SSS2 by M.F. Macrae et al.
- Essential Mathematics SSS2 by A.J.S. Oluwasanmi

#### Second Term
*Source: "MATHEMATICS.docx" / "MATHEMATICS (1).docx" (exact duplicates by file size and content — deduplicated to one entry). No dedicated "2ND TERM S2 MATHEMATICS" file exists, so this pair of files is the primary source for this term.*

- Week 1: Revision — Straight lines: gradient of a straight line, gradient of a curve, drawing of tangents to a curve.
- Week 2: Inequalities — (a) revision of linear inequalities in one variable, (b) solutions of inequalities in two variables, (c) range of values of combined inequalities.
- Week 3: Graphs of linear inequalities in two variables; maximum and minimum values of simultaneous linear inequalities.
- Week 4: Application of linear inequalities to real life; introduction to linear programming.
- Week 5: Algebraic Fractions — simplification of fractions; operations on algebraic fractions.
- Week 6: Equations involving fractions; undefined fractions (e.g. y is undefined when the denominator ax + c = 0).
- Week 7: Review of first half term's work and periodic test.
- Week 8: Fractions (continued) — substitution in fractions; simultaneous equations involving fractions.
- Week 9: Logic — simple and compound statements; logical operations and truth tables; conditional statements and indirect proofs.
- Week 10: Chord properties of circles — perpendicular bisector of a chord; distance of equal chords from the centre of a circle; angles subtended by two equal chords.
- Week 11: Circle Theorems — angle properties of a circle; angle subtended by an arc at the centre is twice that subtended at the circumference; angles in the same segment; angles in a semicircle; opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral.
- Weeks 12–13: Revision and Second Term Examinations.

**Reference Books:** None explicitly listed in this file.

#### Third Term
*Source: "3RD TERM S2 MATHEMATICS.docx".*

- Week 1: Circle Theorems — tangent properties of a circle; angles in the alternate segment; two tangents from an external point.
- Week 2: Trigonometry — derivation of the sine rule and cosine rule and their applications.
- Week 3: Bearing and Distances; Elevation and Depression.
- Week 4: Statistics — class boundaries, class marks, and cumulative frequencies of grouped data; histograms.
- Week 5: Statistics — cumulative frequency curve (Ogive); using the ogive to calculate the median, quartile, percentile and decile.
- Week 6: Review of first half term's work and periodic test.
- Week 7: Statistics — mean, median and mode of grouped data.
- Week 8: Probability — introduction; use of dice, coins and playing cards.
- Week 9: Probability — addition and multiplication rules; mutually exclusive, independent and complementary events; experiments with or without replacement.
- Week 10: Revision.

**Reference Books:**
- New General Mathematics SSS2 by M.F. Macrae et al.
- Essential Mathematics SSS2 by A.J.S. Oluwasanmi
- Exam Focus Mathematics

#### Second Term
*Source: "2ND TERM S3 MATHEMATICS.docx". Note: the source list's week numbers for the last topic were partially lost in extraction ("– 12. Revision and mock examination"); interpreted below as weeks continuing through week 12.*

- Week 1: Calculation of interest on bonds and debentures using logarithm tables; problems on taxes and value-added tax (VAT).
- Week 2: Coordinate Geometry of straight lines — Cartesian coordinate graphs; distance between two points; midpoint of the line joining two points.
- Week 3: Coordinate Geometry of straight lines — gradient and intercepts of a line; angle between two intersecting straight lines and applications.

### SS2

#### First Term
*Source: "1ST TERM S2 FURTHER MATHEMATICS. (1).docx". Note: this file had no separate week-by-week scheme-of-work table; the topic list below was reconstructed from the "WEEK n / TOPIC:" lesson headers that appear through the document, in order.*

- Week 1: Solution to Quadratic Equations — finding a quadratic equation given the sum and product of roots; conditions for equal roots, real roots and no roots.
- Week 2: Tangents and Normals to Curves.
- Week 3: Polynomials.
- Week 4: Polynomials (continued).
- Week 5: Cubic Equations — cubic equations and their factorization; graphs of cubic equations.
- Week 6: Review.
- Week 7: Logical Reasoning — statements, truth values, negation, converse and contrapositive.
- Week 8: Trigonometric Functions.
- Week 9: Graphs of Trigonometric Functions.
- Week 10: Trigonometric Identities and graphs of inverse trigonometric ratios.

**Reference Books:**
- Further Mathematics Project 1 by Tuttuh-Adegun
- Further Mathematics Project 2 by Tuttuh-Adegun
- Additional Mathematics by Godman

#### Second Term
*Source: "2ND TERM S2 FURTHER MATHEMATICS.docx" and "2ND TERM S2 FURTHER MATHEMATICS (1).docx" — confirmed exact duplicates by content and file size; deduplicated to one entry.*

- Week 1: Differentiation — limits of functions and differentiation from first principles; differentiation of polynomials.
- Week 2: Differentiation (continued) — rules of differentiation.
- Week 3: Differentiation of transcendental functions — derivatives of trigonometric and exponential functions.
- Week 4: Application of Differentiation — rate of change, equations of motion, maximum and minimum points and values of functions.
- Week 5: Conic Sections — equation of circles, general equation of circles, finding centre and radius, equation and length of tangents to a circle.
- Week 6: Conic Sections (continued) — the parabola, hyperbola and ellipse.
- Week 7: Review of first half term.
- Week 8: Statistics/Probability — sample space, event space, combination of events, independent and dependent events.
- Week 9: Permutation and Combination.
- Week 10: Dynamics — Newton's Laws of Motion.
- Week 11: Work, Energy, Power, Impulse and Momentum.
- Week 12: Revision and Examination.

**Reference Books:**
- Further Mathematics Project 2 and 3

#### Third Term
*Source: "3RD TERM S2 FURTHER MATHEMATICS.docx".*

- Week 1: Revision of Second Term examination questions.
- Week 2: Projectiles — trajectory of a projectile, greatest height reached, time of flight, range, and projectile motion along an inclined plane.
- Week 3: Binomial Expansion — Pascal's triangle; binomial theorem for negative, positive and fractional powers.
- Week 4: Mechanics — vectors in two and three dimensions; scalar product of vectors in three dimensions.
- Week 5: Vector (cross) product in three dimensions; application of the cross product of two vectors.
- Week 6: Review of half term work.
- Week 7: Integration — concept of indefinite integrals; methods of integration (e.g. algebraic and trigonometric substitution, by parts, and partial fractions).
- Week 8: Integration (continued) — definite integrals; area under a curve.
- Week 9: Integration (continued) — application of integration to kinematics, volumes of solids of revolution, and the trapezium rule.
- Week 10: Correlation and Regression — concept, scatter diagram, regression line, coefficient of regression, rank correlation and product-moment correlation coefficient.
- Week 11: Revision.

**Reference Books:**
- Further Mathematics Project 2 and 3
