All files in this folder are official, freely published Nigerian government curriculum
documents (NERDC — Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council), downloaded
directly from **nerdc.gov.ng**. Nothing here is a pirated copy of a commercial textbook.

## Common Entrance (NCEE + State-level) — past questions

Full official NCEE/state Common Entrance past-question papers are **not** downloaded here
(they're hosted on third-party aggregator sites, not an official archive, so provenance
and copyright status varies). Legit-looking free sources found, for you to check directly
rather than mirror blind:

- NECO NCEE overview & structure: conducted by the National Examinations Council for entry
  into JSS1 of Federal Unity Colleges. Paper I = Maths + Basic Science & Technology +
  English + National Values. Paper II = Quantitative/Vocational Aptitude + Verbal Aptitude.
- [lasu-info.com — NCEE Past Questions & Answers 2011–2024](https://www.lasu-info.com/2018/01/ncee-past-questions-answers-download.html)
- [lasu-info.com — Lagos State Model Colleges Common Entrance Past Questions](https://www.lasu-info.com/2020/01/lagos-state-model-colleges-past-questions.html)
- [schoolngr.com/ncee](https://www.schoolngr.com/ncee) — NCEE past questions + CBT practice
- [classhall.com/common-entrance](https://classhall.com/common-entrance/) — NCEE overview

If we decide to build a Common Entrance track, the cleaner path is sourcing directly from
NECO's official past-question archive (where available) rather than these aggregators.
