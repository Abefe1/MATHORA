# Federal Common Entrance (NCEE) Past Questions

NCEE = National Common Entrance Examination, conducted by NECO for entry into JSS1 of
Federal Government (Unity) Colleges.

**Structure:**
- **Paper I (Achievement Test):** Part A — Mathematics + Basic Science & Technology (BST);
  Part B — English Studies + National Values Education.
- **Paper II (Aptitude Test):** Part A — Quantitative & Vocational Aptitude;
  Part B — Verbal Aptitude.

## Contents (24 files, 2017–2023)

Years covered: 2017, 2018, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 (2019 and 2024 were not posted by the
source site). Each year has 4 files: Paper 1 Questions, Paper 1 Solutions, Paper 2
Questions, Paper 2 Solutions.

## Provenance

Downloaded from Google Drive links published on **lasu-info.com**
([source page](https://www.lasu-info.com/2018/01/ncee-past-questions-answers-download.html)),
a third-party Nigerian education-info aggregator — not NECO's own official archive.
Past exam papers are commonly freely redistributed for study purposes, but note:
- Content ownership (copyright of the actual questions) rests with NECO, not this site.
- No verification was done on answer-key accuracy — treat "Solutions" files as
  unofficial/community-derived unless cross-checked.
- If an authoritative NECO archive becomes available, prefer that over this mirror.
